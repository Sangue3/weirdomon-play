Weirdomon

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

The short version: Weirdomon has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no cookies. It keeps nothing about you once your match is over. Inside Discord it only reads your display name, so your friends see it in the room.

Who we are

Weirdomon is a free browser game made by an independent developer (Discord: sangue, GitHub: Sangue3), who is responsible for the data described here. Contact: prdcgomes7@gmail.com.

Playing alone

A match against the computer runs entirely in your browser. Nothing about it is sent anywhere. The game does not store anything on your device (no cookies, no local storage).

Playing with friends

Online matches go through a small relay server on Cloudflare. It passes between the players in a room what the game needs to stay in sync: the room code, the buttons each player presses, the creature they pick and the name shown in the lobby. All of this lives only in the server's memory while the room is open and is gone when everyone leaves. Nothing is written to a database.

Inside Discord

When you play Weirdomon as a Discord Activity, Discord asks whether the game may identify you. If you agree, the game uses your Discord display name (or username) only to show it to the other players in your Activity. The game also uses the Activity's instance id, so everyone in the same call joins the same room. To get your name, the game exchanges a one-time code from Discord for an access token on our server. The token goes straight back to your game and is never stored. We do not read your messages, servers, friends or email, and we never post anything for you.

If you decline, the game works exactly the same and calls you P1 to P4. You can remove the game's access at any time in Discord, under User Settings → Authorized Apps.

Technical logs

Like any website, the services that host the game see technical data such as your IP address and browser type when you connect. The page is hosted by GitHub Pages and the relay server by Cloudflare. Cloudflare keeps short-lived request logs (a few days) so that we can find and fix errors. We do not use them to identify, profile or track anyone.

What we never do

  • Sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • Show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.
  • Build profiles or make automated decisions about you.

Legal basis and your rights

We process the little data described above because it is necessary to run the game you asked to play (and, for your Discord name, because you agreed to it). Under the GDPR you may ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, or object to its processing, by writing to the contact above. Since we keep nothing after a match, there is usually nothing to hand over. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Portugal, the CNPD).

Children

Weirdomon is not aimed at children under 13. Inside Discord, Discord's own age requirements apply.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.
